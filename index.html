✅
Kiran Dhital Sir (Khagendra) conducted the first Sunday class — Maths. Class ran successfully.
⚠️
Only 2 students joined. Low attendance because students were not pre-enrolled or informed in advance. Students were not interested in joining on Sunday without prior commitment.
💡
Root cause: Class was not created ahead of time for students to enroll. Students need to register/enroll before Sunday — if they commit in advance, attendance and seriousness increases.
Process fix — implement immediately
1️⃣
Create the Sunday class on Abhyas every Thursday — 3 days before. This gives students time to see, enroll, and commit.
2️⃣
Notify all students after class creation — teacher or admin sends message to students informing them of the upcoming Sunday class, subject, and time.
3️⃣
Students pre-enroll/register before Sunday — pre-enrollment signals commitment. Students who have registered are far more likely to attend.
4️⃣
Track enrollment count on Friday/Saturday — if enrollment is low, send a second reminder. Minimum target: 15 students enrolled before Sunday class goes ahead.
